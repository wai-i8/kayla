# Guide media credits

Last verified and downloaded: **20 August 2026**.

These photographs are **illustrative only**. They are not step-by-step medical instructions, do not prove that every detail of the pictured technique is correct, and must be shown with current NHS or Resuscitation Council UK guidance. Do not infer a good latch, safe airway, correct water temperature, or adequate supervision from a photograph alone.

All local files are JPEGs no larger than 960 pixels on either axis. Except where stated otherwise, resizing was performed by Wikimedia Commons' thumbnail service with no crop, retouching, or content alteration.

## Official link-only demonstrations and guidance

The following media remain on their publishers' websites. **Do not download, copy, or rehost them.** No reuse licence is asserted here.

- Breastfeeding positioning and latch: [NHS — Breastfeeding: positioning and attachment](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/breastfeeding-and-bottle-feeding/breastfeeding/positioning-and-attachment/). Includes the midwife video “How do I know if my baby is properly latched?”. Page last reviewed 23 June 2026; video media last reviewed 4 October 2025.
- Changing a nappy: [NHS — How to change your baby's nappy](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/how-to-change-your-babys-nappy/). Includes the midwife video “How do I change a dirty nappy?”. Video media last reviewed 4 October 2025.
- Bathing: [NHS — Bathing your baby (Best Start in Life)](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/baby-basics/caring-for-your-baby/bathing-your-baby/) for current written steps, together with the link-only [NHS video “How do I bath my baby?”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9rg-DEkBQQ). The current written page governs if any detail differs from the older video.
- Baby CPR: [Resuscitation Council UK — Baby and child CPR](https://www.resus.org.uk/baby-cpr), including its embedded [“How to give CPR to a baby (0–1 year)” video](https://youtu.be/oKE9IG7LERg). This page reflects the 2025 guidelines, including calling 999 immediately and the two-thumb encircling compression technique.
- Baby choking: [Resuscitation Council UK — Guidance: Choking](https://www.resus.org.uk/additional-guidance/guidance-choking) and its current paediatric choking algorithm. No older infant-choking video is selected here because several widely circulated videos still show a pre-2025 two-finger chest-thrust technique; use the current RCUK algorithm and a recognised practical first-aid course.

In a real emergency, call **999**, put the phone on loudspeaker, and follow the ambulance call handler's instructions. Online media are refreshers, not a substitute for hands-on first-aid training.

## Selection decisions

- Rejected and removed `WBAMC conducts first SSC after C-section`: although public domain, the adult appears asleep and the baby's face／airway is partly obscured. The Guide links to the official UNICEF UK skin-to-skin demonstration instead.
- Rejected `Infant drinks milk from bottle.jpg`: although CC0, the baby appears too reclined to serve as a clear safe-feeding example. It was replaced by `Fathercare.jpg`.
- Rejected `Baby's first bath.jpg`: although CC BY-SA 2.0, head-and-neck support is not clearly visible and the image could imply that the baby is floating unsupported. It was replaced by the Auckland Museum photograph above.
- Rejected additional bath candidates showing a baby alone in a support seat or bucket, or with ambiguous adult support.

When a CC BY-SA photograph is redistributed or adapted, preserve its attribution, licence link, and ShareAlike terms. If a future crop, overlay, colour adjustment, or other modification is made, record that change here.
